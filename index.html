Redact It – Privacy Policy

Published by The Right to Remain Private · Last updated: September 26, 2026

Redact It is an Android app that finds and removes personal information from text, documents and photos. It is designed so that your content never leaves your phone.

What stays on your device

All processing happens locally on your phone: reading documents, text recognition (OCR) of photos, barcode detection, and the AI model that finds personal information. The text, documents and images you load, and the redacted results you create, are not uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We operate no servers that receive your data.

Network use

The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server, ours or anyone else's. Your documents are never uploaded, and the app has no account, no server of its own and no analytics of its own.

The one-time AI model file (about 4.4 GB) is delivered by Google Play as part of installing the app, using Play's own download service. Google Play may collect information about your device and installation under Google's own privacy policy; that is outside the app. After the model is on your phone, redaction works with no connection at all.

Camera and files

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a document, and only the redacted picture is saved. Files are read only when you pick them. Redacted output is saved or shared only when you tap Save or Share. Sharing sends the redacted file to the app you choose, under that app's own policy.

Third-party components

The app includes Google ML Kit (text recognition and barcode scanning) and Google MediaPipe, which run on your device using models bundled with the app. Because the app has no internet permission, these components cannot send anything off your phone.

The on-device AI model is Google Gemma 3n, provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use (ai.google.dev/gemma/terms) and the Gemma Prohibited Use Policy (ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy). Gemma is a trademark of Google LLC. Redact It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Children

Redact It is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We collect no data from anyone.
